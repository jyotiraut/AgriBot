b = Junk value
c = Expected life of asset ( useful years ).

* Discounting Income $PV = \frac{q}{(1 + r)^n}$
  Where, 
  Pv = Present Value of the future amount
  q = Amount to be spent at a future date
  r = Rate of interest
  n = Number of years in future when money is to be spent

  Net Present Value $(NPV) = \sum_{t1}^{tn} \frac{Bt - Ct}{(1 + i)^t}$
  Where, 
  $\text{B}_t$ = Benefits in each year ( benefits at year t)
  $\text{C}_t$ = Costs in each year or at year t
  t = 1,2,......n ( number of years )
  i = Interest rate or discount rate

* Internal Rate of Return $\text{(IRR)} = Li + \frac{(Hi - Li)NPVatLi}{NPVatLi - NPVatHi}$
  Where 
  Hi = higher discount rate
  Li = Lower discount rate.
